# The 3D view

**View > Thread Look** with **3D (light and shade)** shows the
stitches as they look on the fabric. The pictures that the program
makes (the design picture, the approval sheet, the product, the
library and the thumbnails of the file manager) have the same look.

## What it shows

- **Height**: thread on thread is higher. A satin column is round
  across, underlay lifts the stitches on it, and puff stands up.
- **Sheen**: an area is lighter or darker by the direction of its
  stitches to the light. This shows a satin as a satin and the pattern
  of a fill.
- **Shadows and gaps**: a high part puts a shadow on the fabric and on
  lower stitches, and the gaps between stitches are darker.
- **The fabric**: the weave, the loops of a towel or the grain of
  leather at its real size, with a dent at each needle point (see
  [Fabrics, styles and templates](fabrics-and-styles)).
- **What is not thread**: appliqué fabric, rhinestones, the holes of
  cutwork, lace with no fabric, padding and the batting of a quilt.

## The kinds of thread

Each thread has a **Kind** and a **Weight** (the Threads panel, see
[Colors and threads](colors-and-threads)). Rayon shines more than
polyester, cotton and wool are matte, metallic thread has small glints
when you zoom in, and glow-in-the-dark thread shows in its own light
with **Lights off**. A 60 weight thread is thinner than a 40 weight
thread.

## The light

**View > Lighting** and the Thread Look menu have the presets **From the
top left** (the default), **Flat from above**, **Low from the side** and
**Two lights**, and the direction, the height and the strength of the
light. Hold **L** and drag on the design to move the light. A low light
from the side shows each height and each gap.

## Check the look

The **Check** group of the View tab has the **Tilt View** (the
embroidery from an angle, with the heights made larger if you like), the
**Magnifier** (20 times), **Compare 3D**, **Before and After**, and the
checks **Fabric Shows Through** and **High Thread**. See [Checking a
design](checking).

## Quality

The **Quality** in the Thread Look menu sets how much of the view the
program makes:

- **Fast**: the stitches one by one, with light and shade. For a slow
  computer.
- **Good**: the surface of the stitches, with no shadows from the
  heights.
- **Best**: all of the surface.

At the first start the program makes a short measure of the computer
and chooses the quality. With the software renderer (no graphics card)
it starts with **Fast**. You can change it at each time.

## Pictures for customers and shops

**Output Design** (**Design picture (PNG)**) writes the picture with the
3D look at 150, 254, 300 or 600 dots for each inch, on the fabric color
or with **No background** (for a web shop). **Sew-out animation (GIF)**
writes an animated picture of the design as it sews.
