# Pictures and Picture to design

A picture can become embroidery in three ways:

- **Picture to Design**: a wizard makes the objects for you, step by step.
- **Trace** and **click to stitch**: the program makes vector shapes or
  objects from the color areas of a backdrop picture.
- **By hand**: you draw the objects on the backdrop picture with the
  Digitize tools.

A logo or a drawing with a few flat colors gives the best result.

## The backdrop picture

**Artwork > Backdrop Picture** puts a PNG, JPEG, BMP, TIFF or GIF picture
under the design, dimmed, so you can draw on it. A photo from a phone
comes in upright. The picture is locked, so a click on it does not move
it.

The **Backdrop** part of Object Properties (click an empty place of the
canvas) has:

- **Show**, **Lock**, and the dimming of the picture.
- **Width** (the height follows), **Center X** and **Y**.
- **Replace…** loads another picture in the same place.
- **Edit in Another Program** opens the picture in the picture program
  of the computer. Save it there: the backdrop changes here.
- **Remove**.

The backdrop does not sew.

## Picture tools

**Artwork > Picture Tools** has:

- **Adjust picture**: **Brightness**, **Contrast** and **Sharpen**. Click
  **Apply**.
- **Prepare colors**: fewer **Colors**, **Remove specks under** (areas
  with fewer pixels take the color round them) and **Clean edges**. The
  picture is then ready to trace or to digitize by hand. **Add these
  threads** adds a design thread for each color.
- **Crop Box**, **Crop Oval** and **Crop Shape** (Artwork > Picture) cut
  the picture to a box, an oval or a shape that you click.

## Trace and click to stitch

- **Trace Picture**: each color area of the backdrop becomes a vector
  shape in its color (not the background). **Smoothing** sets how far
  the curves can go from the pixel edges; **Corners** sets which turns
  stay sharp; **Leave out areas under** skips small areas. Then use
  **Artwork > Convert** to make embroidery of the shapes.
- **Click to Stitch**: click a color area of the picture. It becomes a
  **Click Fill** (with its holes), a **Fill, No Holes**, a **Click Satin**
  (satin columns along its middle), a **Center Line** (a running line
  along its middle) or a **Click Outline** (a running line round it).
  Choose with the buttons next to the tool.

## Picture to design

**Artwork > Picture to Design** (also on the start page) opens the
wizard. Each step shows its result on the right. Click **Next ›** and
**‹ Back** to go between the steps. In the preview, scroll to zoom and
drag with the right or middle button to pan.

### Picture

Choose the picture, the **Design width**, and what to **Make**:

- **Color areas** (a logo, a drawing): fills, satins and outlines.
- **Photo stitch** (a portrait, a photo): rows or scribbles whose density
  follows the light and dark of the picture.
- **Cross Stitch** (a chart of crosses): a cross in each cell of a grid.

Crop the picture first (Artwork > Picture) if you want only a part of it.

### Background

Remove the parts that must not sew:

- **Smart > Cut out subject**: drag a box round the subject, with a
  little background all round it. Then paint with **Mark keep** over
  parts of the subject that went, and with **Mark remove** over
  background that stayed. Each mark cuts again.
- **By Color**: click a color to remove it, **Only the area I click** or
  **This color everywhere**, with a **Tolerance**. **Remove the edge
  color** removes the most common color at the edges of the picture: it
  works well on a plain background only.
- **By hand**: the **Erase brush** and the **Restore brush**, or a
  **Box** or **Lasso** with **Erase inside**, **Keep only inside** and
  **Restore inside**.
- **Clean up**: **Shrink the edge by** clears a band at the edge of the
  cut-out (the mixed colors of the old background); **Remove bits under**
  removes small bits that stay and fills small holes.

**Undo** and **Start again** go back.

### Simplify

- **Detail** (mm a pixel): smaller gives more detail; larger gives
  simpler shapes and fewer small areas.
- **Remove specks under**: areas smaller than this take the color round
  them.
- **Smooth noise** removes grain. **Even out** makes flat areas even
  (JPEG blocks, the grain of a scan) and keeps the edges sharp.
- **Clean edges**.
- **Colors from the thread chart**: the colors are threads of the chart
  of the Threads panel.

### Colors

Set the number of colors. Clear a color to leave it out (no stitches), or
use **Merge into** to join it with another color.

### Stitches

For each color, in sew order (the first sews first, under the others),
select the stitch: **Auto (satin if narrow)**, **Fill**, **Satin**,
**Outline**, or **Artwork only (no stitches)**. **Sew earlier** and
**Sew later** change the order. The settings:

- **Overlap**: fills grow by half of this, so colors that touch overlap
  and no fabric shows between them.
- **Auto satin up to**: Auto makes a satin of a long area narrower than
  this.
- **Details on top up to**: a small detail inside an area sews on top of
  it, and the area has no hole there.
- **Small areas under**: Auto makes a smaller area a satin, or a run if
  it is very narrow.
- **Fill along each area**: each fill sews along the long side of its
  area, and fills that touch get angles 30° or more apart.

With **Auto**, thin, long, dark areas (the outlines of a drawing) sew
last, as runs or narrow satins.

### Photo stitch

Choose the **Style** (**Rows**, **Color rows** or **Scribble**), the
**Row spacing** or **Point spacing**, the **Row angle**, the **Contrast**
and **Brightness**, and the **Thread colors**.

### Cross stitch

Choose the **Cell size** and the number of thread colors.
**Three-quarter crosses at the edges** makes slanted edges smoother.
Print the chart with **Specialty > Cross Stitch > Chart**.

### Finish

The step shows the numbers of the result and the **Checks**: many trims,
very small objects, long stitches, thread that builds up, and two colors
that are almost the same. A check can have a button that changes the
settings to fix it. Then click **Finish**: the objects go into the
design. **Group the objects** and **Hide the backdrop picture** are
options. Undo takes all the objects back.

While the wizard makes a preview, the preview shows **Working…** and the
last picture. You can change more settings; the wizard then uses the
newest settings.

## Tips

- A photo has many small areas, and each needs a trim. Use **Cut out
  subject**, less detail and larger specks, or **Photo stitch**. See
  [Questions and problems](troubleshooting).
- Picture to design makes a start. Check the sew order and the objects
  after, and change them as you need.
