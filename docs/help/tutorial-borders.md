# Tutorial: borders, motifs and families

In this tutorial you put a decorative border round a shape, give a line
and a fill a motif, and add a clipart item that the program makes from a
family.

## Open the start design

Click [Open the start design](sample:decor-shapes). It has a gold circle
(a fill) and a navy line under it.

## Add a border

1. Click the circle to select it.
2. Click **Digitize > Decorate > Borders**. The **Borders** window opens.
   It shows a picture of each border.
3. Type `hearts` in the search field, and click **Hearts**.
4. Set **Space round the objects** to 3 mm.
5. Click **Add the border**. A border of hearts goes round the circle.
   With nothing selected, the border goes round the whole design.

![The Borders window](borders-window)

**Shape** gives the border another shape (for example **Rectangle**
round a circle), and **Size of the stitches** makes the stitches of the
border larger or smaller.

6. Close the **Borders** window.

## Give a line a motif

A motif is a small shape that repeats along a line (a motif run), along a
column, or in rows over an area (a motif fill).

1. Click the navy line to select it.
2. Click **Edit > Stitch Types > Motifs**. The **Motifs** window opens.
3. Click **Scallop**, then **Use for the selection**. The line sews as a
   row of scallops.
4. Click the circle to select it. Click **Bead**, then **Use for the
   selection**. The circle sews as rows of beads.

Object Properties sets the **Motif length** and the **Motif height**.

## A clipart item from a family

A family makes many items from one shape: a star with 4 to 8 points, a
flower with more or fewer petals. Each item is a design of its own.

1. In the **Motifs** window, click **Families…**. The **Families** window
   opens.
2. Click **Clipart**, then **Six-point star** (a built-in item of the
   family Star).
3. Change **Points** or **Inner part** to change the star. The picture
   shows the item.

![The Families window](borders-families)

4. Click **Insert**. The star goes in the middle of the view: a fill and
   an outline.
5. Close the windows. Click **View > Show > Thread Look**, and select
   **Thread Look** in its menu.

![The circle with its border, the scallops and the star](borders-stitches)

## If it does not look right

- **The border goes round everything.** Nothing was selected. Undo, select
  the shape, and add the border again.
- **Use for the selection is gray.** Select a line, a column or a fill
  first.
- **The motifs are too small or too dense.** Make the **Motif length**
  and the **Motif height** larger in Object Properties.
