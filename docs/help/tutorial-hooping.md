# Tutorial: a design in two hoopings

In this tutorial you sew a design that is larger than the hoop, in two
parts. You hoop the fabric two times, and registration marks show where
the second part goes.

## Open the start design

1. Click [Open the start design](sample:hooping-banner). It is a row of
   five dots, 380 mm wide.
2. On the **Design** tab, select your machine and your largest hoop.
   Orange outlines show the objects that go out of the hoop.

## Make the hoopings

1. Click **Hooping > Hooping > Hooping Mode**. The program makes the
   fewest hoopings that cover the design (**Auto Layout**), with a split
   line between them. Each hooping has a number and a color. In a hoop
   of 260 × 200 mm, the row needs two hoopings.
2. If a split line goes through an object that you do not want to cut,
   drag the line or its nodes to another place. Objects that do not fit
   one hooping are cut at the split line.
3. In the **Hooping > Options** panel, keep **Registration marks** on:
   the machine sews crosses where the two hoopings meet.

![The two hoopings of the row](hooping-layout)

## Check the hoopings

1. Click **Hooping > Output > Preview Hoopings**. The window shows each
   hooping with its stitches, colors and warnings. Fix each warning, for
   example a hooping whose stitches go out of the field.
2. Click **Preview Hoopings** again to close it.

![The Preview Hoopings window](hooping-preview)

## Write the files

Click **Hooping > Output > Output Hoopings** and select a folder. The
program writes a machine file for each hooping (name_1, name_2), the
files of Output Design, and a hooping sheet (PDF) with the order and the
places.

## Sew the hoopings

1. Hoop the fabric for hooping 1 and sew `name_1`. The machine sews the
   registration crosses last.
2. Move the fabric in the hoop for hooping 2, as the hooping sheet shows.
   Hooping 2 must cover the crosses of hooping 1.
3. Open `name_2` on the machine. Move the design on the machine until its
   first crosses are on the crosses that you sewed.
4. Sew `name_2`. Pull out the crosses after.

## If it does not look right

- **The two parts do not meet.** The fabric moved when you hooped it
  again. Use a firm backing that is larger than both hoopings, and align
  the crosses with care.
- **Auto Layout does nothing.** The design has the open field as its
  hoop. Select a hoop on the **Design** tab.
- **A hooping has a warning.** Open **Preview Hoopings**, and click the
  hooping to see it.
