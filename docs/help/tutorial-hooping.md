# Tutorial: a design in two hoopings

In this tutorial you sew a design that is larger than the hoop, in two
parts. It takes about 20 minutes.

## Make a large design

1. On the **Design** tab, select your machine and a hoop, for example a
   hoop of 240 × 150 mm.
2. Make or open a design that is wider than the hoop, for example
   lettering 350 mm wide. Orange outlines show the objects that go out of
   the hoop.

## Make the hoopings

1. Click **Hooping > Hooping Mode**.
2. Click **Auto Layout**. The program makes the fewest hoopings that
   cover the design, with a split line between them. Each hooping has a
   number and a color.
3. If the split line goes through a letter, drag the line or its nodes to
   a place between two letters. Objects that do not fit one hooping are
   cut at the split line.
4. In the **Options** panel, keep **Registration marks** on: the machine
   sews crosses where the two hoopings meet.

## Check the hoopings

Click **Preview Hoopings**. It shows each hooping with its stitches,
colors and warnings. Fix each warning, for example a hooping whose
stitches go out of the field.

## Write the files

Click **Output Hoopings** and select a folder. The program writes a
machine file for each hooping (name_1, name_2), the files of Output
design, and a hooping sheet (PDF) with the order and the places.

## Sew the hoopings

1. Hoop the fabric for hooping 1 and sew `name_1`. The machine sews the
   registration crosses last.
2. Move the fabric in the hoop for hooping 2, as the hooping sheet shows.
   Hooping 2 must cover the crosses of hooping 1.
3. Open `name_2` on the machine. Move the design on the machine until its
   first crosses are on the crosses that you sewed.
4. Sew `name_2`. Pull out the crosses after.
