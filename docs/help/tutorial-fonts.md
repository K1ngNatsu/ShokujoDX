# Tutorial: choose a font and a size

In this tutorial you find a font in the **Fonts** window, read its size
range and its quality score, and see what to do when text is too small
or too large for a font.

## Two kinds of fonts

- **Embroidery fonts** have letters that are ready-made embroidery: satin
  columns and fills in a good sew order. 68 come with the program. Each
  has a size range and a quality score.
- **TrueType fonts** (and OpenType fonts) are the fonts of your computer,
  and some fonts of the program. The program makes the stitches of each
  letter from its outline. They have no quality score: look at the
  stitches before you sew.

Use an embroidery font when you can.

## Find a font

1. Click [Open the start design](sample:lettering-start).
2. Click **Lettering > Lettering > Fonts**. The **Fonts** window opens.
3. At **Sort by**, click **Quality**. The fonts with the best quality
   score are first. The score (0 to 100) says how well the letters of a
   font sew.
4. Type `Pacifico` in the search field, and click **Pacifico** in the
   list. The window shows a preview of the letters, the size range (10 to
   300 mm, the height of the capital letters) and the quality score.

![The Fonts window](fonts-window)

5. Close the **Fonts** window.

## Make lettering in the size range

1. Click **Lettering > Lettering > Lettering**, and click the
   construction point.
2. Type `Sam`.
3. In the **Font** list, select **Pacifico**. The list has a search field
   at its top.
4. Set **Height** to 20 mm, and click **OK**.

Object Properties shows the size range of the font under **Height**.

## Satin or fill

The letters of Pacifico are satin columns. A satin column longer than
about 12 mm is loose, so for large text the font also has each letter as
fills. The **Stitch type** in Object Properties chooses:

- **Automatic**: satin columns up to the height where they are too long,
  fills above it.
- **Satin**: always satin columns.
- **Fill**: always fills.

1. Set **Stitch type** to **Fill**. The letters sew as fills.
2. Click **View > Show > Thread Look**, and select **Thread Look** in its
   menu.

![The name with fill letters](fonts-fill)

3. Push **Esc**, click **Edit > Select > Select All**, and set **Stitch
   type** to **Automatic** again.

## Text that is too small

1. Set **Height** in Object Properties to 6 mm. The size range shows in
   the color of a warning, and Design Info has a warning: the letters are
   smaller than the size range. Small satin columns are too narrow to
   sew well.
2. Click **Lettering > Lettering > Fonts**, and click **For small text**.
   The list shows the fonts for letters from 4 mm high.
3. Click **Atkinson Hyperlegible**, then **Use for the selected
   lettering**. The warning goes.

![Fonts for small text](fonts-small)

**For large text** shows the fill fonts: their letters are fills, for
letters from 15 mm high.

## More fonts

- **More Fonts…** in the **Fonts** window opens the page of the program
  on the internet. The font packages there have more embroidery fonts.
  Add a package with **File > My Assets** (**Import a package**).
- A TrueType font of your computer can become an embroidery font with a
  size range and a quality score: see [Tutorial: an embroidery font of
  your own](tutorial-own-font).

## If it does not look right

- **The font is not in the list.** Check the search field of the list: it
  keeps its text. Clear it.
- **Design Info shows "smaller than the size range".** Make the text
  larger, or use a font for small text.
- **Large satin letters look loose.** Set **Stitch type** to
  **Automatic** or **Fill**.
