# Tutorial: a logo by hand

In this tutorial you put stitches on a logo picture by hand: a leaf with
a hole, a stem and a vein. You learn the three kinds of objects: a fill
for an area, a satin column for a narrow band, and a line for a thin
line. Picture to Design does this for you, but by hand you choose each
shape, its stitches and its sew order.

You need no file. The start design has the picture of the logo.

## Open the start design

Click [Open the start design](sample:logo-backdrop). The logo shows
dimmed under the design: it is a backdrop picture, and it does not sew.

![The leaf logo as a backdrop picture](logo-by-hand-start)

Each drawing tool works the same way:

- A left click puts a **corner**: the line turns sharply there.
- A right click puts a **curve point**: the line goes through it in a
  smooth curve.
- **Enter** finishes a part. **Backspace** removes the last point.
  **Esc** stops the tool.

The status line tells you what to click next.

## The leaf: a fill with a hole

1. In the **Threads** strip, click Green.
2. Click **Digitize > Fills > Complex Fill**.
3. Click round the outline of the leaf: a left click at the stem and at
   the tip, right clicks on the round sides. Push **Enter**.
4. Click round the hole with right clicks (four points are enough). Push
   **Enter**.
5. Push **Enter** again: the leaf has no more holes. The fill sews round
   the hole.
6. Push **Esc** two times: the tool stops, and the leaf is not selected.
   (A thread that you click goes to the selected objects too.)

## The stem: a satin column

1. In the **Threads** strip, click Brown.
2. Click **Digitize > Columns > Column**.
3. Click along one side of the stem: a left click at each end, a right
   click in the middle. Push **Enter**.
4. Click along the other side in the same direction. Push **Enter**. The
   satin fills the space between the two sides.
5. Push **Esc** two times.

## The vein: a line

1. In the **Threads** strip, click Dark green.
2. Click **Digitize > Lines > Line**.
3. Click the start of the vein, a curve point in the middle (right
   click), and its end. Push **Enter**.
4. Push **Esc** two times.

![The three objects on the picture](logo-by-hand-objects)

## Look at the stitches

Click **View > Show > Thread Look**, and select **Thread Look** in its
menu. The leaf sews first, then the stem and the vein on top of it.

![The leaf with the thread look](logo-by-hand-stitches)

## Check it

- The status line shows **Objects: 3** and **Colors: 3**, and no
  warning.
- **View > Stitch Cursor > Stitch Player** plays the sew-out.
- To move a point, click **Digitize > Select > Reshape**, then drag the
  point.

## Your own logo

Click **Artwork > Import > Backdrop Picture**, and select your picture
(PNG, JPEG, BMP, TIFF or GIF). Object Properties shows the backdrop
settings when nothing is selected: its width, its place and its
strength. Draw over it as in this tutorial. Large areas are fills,
bands up to about 10 mm wide are satin columns, and thin lines are runs.

## If it does not look right

- **The fill covers the hole.** The hole was not a part of the fill:
  select the leaf, click **Edit > Shape > Add Hole**, and click round the
  hole.
- **The satin is twisted.** You clicked the two sides in opposite
  directions. Click **Undo**, and click both sides from the same end.
- **An object has the thread of the next one.** It was selected when you
  clicked the thread. Click **Undo**, push **Esc**, and click the thread
  again.
