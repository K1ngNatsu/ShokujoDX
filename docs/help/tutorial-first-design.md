# Tutorial: your first design

In this tutorial you make a badge: a gold circle with a navy satin
border. You use the parts of the program that each design needs: a tool
that draws, the thread colors, the stitch player and the machine file.

You need no file and no machine. The program makes the start design.

## Open the start design

1. Click [Open the start design](sample:first-steps). The design opens in
   a new tab, on your machine and in your hoop.
2. The design has two construction points. They show two corners of the
   circle. Construction points do not sew.

![The start design: two construction points in the hoop](first-design-start)

The **Threads** strip under the canvas shows the two threads of the
design: Gold and Navy.

## Draw the circle

1. In the **Threads** strip, click Gold. New objects get this thread.
2. Click **Digitize > Shapes > Ellipse**. (On a small screen, a panel
   of the ribbon can show as one button with its name. Click it to see
   its commands.)
3. Hold **Ctrl**, and drag from one construction point to the other.
   **Ctrl** makes a circle.
4. Push **Esc** to stop the tool.

The circle is a fill: rows of stitches cover it.

![The gold circle](first-design-circle)

## Add a satin border

1. Click **Digitize > Select > Select**, then click the circle. The
   circle is selected.
2. Click **Artwork > Borders and Offsets > Add Border**. The **Offsets**
   window opens, with **Border** set.
3. Click **Add outlines**. A satin border goes round the circle, and the
   border is selected.
4. In the **Threads** strip, click Navy. The border gets the navy thread.
5. Close the **Offsets** window.

![The Offsets window](first-design-offsets)

## Look at the stitches

1. Click **View > Show > Thread Look**, and select **Thread Look** in its
   menu. The stitches show as threads.

![The badge with the thread look](first-design-badge)

2. Click **View > Stitch Cursor > Stitch Player**. A bar shows under the
   canvas. Click its play button to see the sew order: first the gold
   fill, then the navy border.

## Check it

The status line under the canvas shows:

- **Objects: 2** and **Colors: 2**;
- **Stitches:** about 4,400;
- **Size:** 65 × 65 mm. The circle is 60 mm wide, and the border is
  outside it.

## Save the design and write the machine file

1. Click **Save** (or push **Ctrl+S**), and give the design a name. The
   program saves the design as an `.embproj` file. Keep this file: it has
   the shapes and the settings, so you can change the design later.
2. Click **Design > Output > Export**. The window shows the file format of
   your machine, the size, the stitches and the colors. Click **Export…**,
   and save the machine file. Your machine sews from this file.

The next tutorial sends the machine file to your machine.

## If it does not look right

- **The circle is not round.** You did not hold Ctrl. Click **Undo**, and
  draw the circle again.
- **Add outlines does not work.** No object is selected. Select the
  circle with the **Select** tool first.
- **The border is gold.** Select the border, and click Navy in the
  **Threads** strip.
- **The stitches show as thin lines.** The thread look is off. Turn it on
  again in the **Thread Look** menu.
