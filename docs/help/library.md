# The design library and clipart

## The design library

**Design > Design Library** (or **File > Design Library**) shows the
Library panel: the designs in your design folders and their subfolders,
with a picture of each.

- **Add a library folder** adds a folder. **Remove** takes it out of the
  library (no file is deleted). **Scan again** reads the new and changed
  designs.
- **Thumbnails** or **List** shows the designs. In the list, click a
  column title (Name, Format, Size, Stitches, Colors, Folder) to sort by
  it; click again for the other way.
- **Search** finds designs by the words of the name, file, folder, notes,
  threads or format. Filters narrow the search:

| Filter | Finds |
|---|---|
| `format:vp3,dst` | designs in these formats |
| `folder:caps` | designs in a folder with this name |
| `colors:<4` | designs with fewer than 4 colors |
| `stitches:1000-5000` | designs with 1000 to 5000 stitches |
| `width:<100`, `height:>50` | designs of a size in mm |
| `fits:crown` | designs that fit a hoop |
| `error:yes` | designs that cannot be read |
| `"two words"` | the two words together |

With designs selected:

- **Open** (or a double-click) opens them.
- **New from selected** makes a new design that is a copy of the selected
  design. The file does not change.
- **Show in File Explorer** shows the file.
- **Output selected** makes the files of the output profile (machine
  files, worksheets, pictures) for each selected design, in a folder.
- **Send to USB** sends the selected designs to the USB stick of the
  machine of the open design, in its format.

To print or list the found designs:

- **Catalog (PDF)**: a picture of each design with its numbers, 20 on a
  page.
- **Design list (CSV)**: the designs and their numbers, for a
  spreadsheet.

## Clipart

Clipart items are pieces of designs that you use again: frames, badges,
small pictures. The program has some items, and you can add your own.

- **Edit > Clipart** shows the Clipart panel. **Search clipart** finds an
  item by its name or keywords.
- Double-click an item, or drag it onto the design, to insert it.
  **Insert** does the same.
- **Edit > Save As Clipart** keeps the selected objects as an item, with a
  **Name** and **Keywords** (words that the search finds, for example
  "rose flower red").
- **Show the clipart folder** opens the folder; **Read the clipart folder
  again** finds new files there.

Your clipart is one of your assets: **My Assets** exports and imports it.
See [Fabrics, styles and templates](fabrics-and-styles).
