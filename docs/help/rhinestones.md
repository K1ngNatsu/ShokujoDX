# Rhinestones

Rhinestones (hotfix stones) do not sew. You put them on a template,
pick them up with transfer tape, and press them onto the fabric with
heat. The program places the stones and writes the template.

## Place stones

- **Stone Run**: a line of stones. Left click: corner; right click: curve
  point; Enter: finish. The stones go from end to end.
- **Stone**: one stone for each click.
- **Stone Fill**: a shape filled with stones. Click its outline; Enter or
  a click on the first point finishes it.

The property bar sets the stone: the size (SS6 to SS34, with the
diameter), the color, and the **Gap** between the edges of two stones.
A fill has **Rows** (at an angle; each other row moves half a stone, a
honeycomb) or **Rings**.

## Make stones from objects

Select shapes, lines or lettering, then:

- **Outline to Stones**: a run of stones along the outlines (rhinestone
  lettering).
- **Center Line to Stones**: one line of stones along the middle of each
  narrow stroke.
- **Fill with Stones**: stones that fill the areas.

## Change the stones

- Select a stone object to set its stone in Object Properties.
- Stones that overlap show red. **Remove Overlaps** (Object Properties)
  leaves out the stones that overlap stones placed before them.
- **To Single Stones**: the selected runs and fills become single stones
  at the same places. Reshape then moves each stone; Delete in Reshape
  removes one.
- **My stones** in the stone list keeps the stones that you use often
  (size and color), for all designs. **Keep this stone** adds one.
- **Rhinestones** (Specialty tab) shows or hides the stones on the
  canvas. Hidden stones still go in the template and the worksheet.

## The template

**Specialty > Template** writes the rhinestone template at real size: a
hole for each stone, a little larger than the stone, and registration
marks.

- **SVG** or **DXF** for a cutter (cut the template from template
  material), or **PDF** on paper.
- **Template holes +** (the property bar of the stone tools, and Output
  design) sets how much larger a hole is than its stone. Thick or soft template material needs more; 0.2 to 0.4 mm is
  usual.
- Output design can write the template too.

## Template marks

A design with stones and stitches needs the stones in the correct place
on the embroidery. **Specialty > Template Marks** adds four crosses that
sew last, in their own color, outside the stones:

1. Sew the design. The machine sews the crosses last.
2. Put the template (and the transfer tape with the stones) on the
   crosses.
3. Press the stones.
4. Pull the crosses out after the press.

Click **Template Marks** again to move the crosses back to the corners of
the stones. The worksheet has the order of work for rhinestones.
