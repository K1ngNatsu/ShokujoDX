# Designs larger than the hoop

A design that is larger than the hoop sews in parts. Each part is a
**hooping**: you sew it, move the fabric in the hoop, and sew the next
part. The **Hooping** tab makes the hoopings. See [Tutorial: a design in
two hoopings](tutorial-hooping).

## Hooping mode

**Hooping > Hooping Mode** shows the hoopings on the canvas, each with
its number and color. In hooping mode:

- Drag the field of a hooping to move it.
- Drag a split line or its nodes to change it.
- Click a hooping or a split line to select it; **Delete** removes it.
- **Esc** ends hooping mode.

## Make the hoopings

- **Auto Layout** makes the fewest hoopings of the hoop that cover the
  design, with split lines between them. A multi-position hoop gets a
  hooping for each of its positions.
- **Add Hooping** adds a hooping at the middle of the canvas. **Turn Hooping** turns the selected hooping 90° (the hoop goes on the fabric
  the other way). **Delete** deletes the selected hooping or split line.
- **Split Line** draws a split line: click its points, then **Enter** or
  a double-click. An object that does not fit one hooping is cut at the
  split line.
- **Remove Split Lines** removes all split lines; each zone then goes to
  one hooping only. **Remove Hoopings** removes all hoopings.

Put split lines where a cut does not show, for example between two
objects, or across a fill and not across lettering.

## Options

The **Options** panel of the Hooping tab has:

- **Hoop**: the hoop of the hoopings.
- **Margin**: the space between the field edge and the stitches.
- **Field overlap**: how far the fields of two hoopings overlap at least
  (for Auto layout). The registration marks go there.
- **Join overlap**: how far a cut fill goes into the next hooping, so no
  fabric shows at the join.
- **Registration marks**: crosses where two hoopings meet. The first
  hooping sews them last, the next one first. Put the marks of the new
  hooping on the marks that you sewed, then sew.

## Check and write the files

- **Preview Hoopings** shows a picture of each hooping with its
  stitches, colors and warnings. The hoopings sew in the order of their
  numbers.
- **Output Hoopings** writes a machine file for each hooping (name_1,
  name_2, …), with the files of Output design, and a hooping sheet (PDF)
  with the order and the places.

## How to sew the hoopings

1. Mark the center lines of the design on the fabric, or use the hoop
   template (Design > Hoop Template).
2. Hoop the fabric for hooping 1 and sew its file.
3. Move the fabric in the hoop to the place of hooping 2. The hooping
   sheet shows where it goes.
4. On the machine, move the design so that its first registration marks
   are on the marks that hooping 1 sewed.
5. Sew hooping 2, and so on.

Quilt blocks that are larger than the hoop also sew in hoopings: see
[Quilting](quilting).
