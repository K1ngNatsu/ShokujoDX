# Tutorial: a quote with a product picture

In this tutorial you show a customer the club crest on a polo shirt, give
the price of 24 shirts, and make the approval sheet with a picture of the
shirt.

## Open the start design

Click [Open the club crest](sample:club-crest).

## The design on the product

1. Click **Design > Product > Product**. The **Product** window opens.
2. Choose **Polo shirt** in the **Product** list.
3. Click the color **Light blue** under the color of the product.
4. Under **Put the design at**, click **Left chest**. The polo shirt moves
   under the design, so that the design is on the left chest.
5. Click **Zoom to product**, and close the **Product** window.
6. Click **View > Workspace > Grid** to hide the grid. Click it again
   later to show the grid.

![The club crest on a polo shirt](quote-product)

The product is at its real size, and it does not sew. If the design is
larger than the usual size for its place, the **Product** window says so.
**Design > Product > Show Product** shows or hides the product.

## The prices

1. Click **Design > Orders > Quote**. The **Quote** window opens.
2. Under **Prices**, set the prices of your shop, for example:
   - **Each 1000 stitches**: 1.20
   - **Lowest price for each piece**: 4
   - **Colors in the price**: 3, and **Each added color**: 0.25
   - **Setup**: 15, once for the order
   - **Digitizing**: 25, once for a new design
3. Click **Add a Quantity Price**. From 12 pieces, the embroidery price is
   10 % less.

The price list is kept for the next quotes. **New List** makes a copy of
the list, for example one list for shirts and one for caps.

## The order

1. Set the **Quantity** to 24.
2. Select **New design (add the digitizing price)**: you made the design
   for this customer.
3. Click **Prices** to fold the prices.

The window shows each line of the price, the total and the price of each
piece. The **Run time** under it is the time to sew the order on the
machine of the design.

![The Quote window](quote-window)

**Save Quote Sheet** writes a PDF page for the customer: the design
picture, the lines of the price, the total and lines to sign.

4. Close the **Quote** window.

## The approval sheet

1. Click **Design > Output > Print Preview**.
2. Click **Approval sheet**. The first page has the design picture, its
   size and threads, the order and the price.
3. Click ▶ (the next page). The second page shows the design on the polo
   shirt.

![The product page of the approval sheet](quote-approval)

**Save PDF…** saves the sheet. Send it to the customer to approve.
**Design > Output > Output Design** can also make the approval sheet with
the machine files (**With the product**).

## If it does not look right

- **The Quote window says "Set the prices of the price list first".** Open
  **Prices** and set a price.
- **The approval sheet has no product page.** Choose a product in the
  **Product** window first.
- **The design is too large for the place.** Make it smaller (**Arrange**
  tab), or choose a larger place.
