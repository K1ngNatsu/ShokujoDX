# The canvas and the selection

The canvas shows the hoop and the design. The top of the canvas is the
back of the machine.

## Zoom and pan

- Turn the mouse wheel to zoom at the pointer.
- Drag with the middle or the right mouse button to move the view.
- Double-click the mouse wheel, or push **Home**, to see the hoop and all
  the design (**View > Zoom Extents**).
- **+** and **-** zoom in and out (**View > Zoom In**, **Zoom Out**).
  Options sets the zoom step.
- **View > Zoom** also has **Zoom 100 %** (the real size; calibrate the
  screen in Options), **Zoom to Hoop**, **Zoom to Selection**, the
  **Zoom** tool (drag a box), a list of zoom factors, and ½× and 2×.
- **Previous View** goes back to the view before the last zoom.

## More views

- **View > Overview** shows a small window with all of the design. Drag
  in it to move the view.
- **View > Views** has:
  - **Split View**: a second view next to the first, with its own zoom
    and the thread look. Use it to see the real size and a detail at the
    same time.
  - **New View**: up to three more views of the design.
  - **Other Design**: another open design next to this one. Click **Edit
    this design** to work on it.

## Select objects

- Click an object to select it. **Ctrl+click** adds or removes an object.
  **Shift+click** selects all objects between two objects in the sew order.
- Drag a box to the **right**: it selects the objects that are fully in the
  box. Drag a box to the **left**: it selects the objects that it touches.
- **Tab** and **Shift+Tab** select the next and the previous object in the
  sew order.
- **Ctrl+A** selects all objects.
- **Lasso**, **Poly Select** and **Line Select** (Digitize > Select)
  select with a line that you draw: the objects inside a freehand line,
  inside a polygon, or the objects that a line crosses.
- **Edit > Select** selects **By Color** or **By Type**, and **At Stitch Cursor**.
- A click on one object of a group selects all of the group.

## Move and change the selection

- Drag the selection to move it. The corner handles scale it; the round
  handle turns it.
- The arrow keys move the selection a little. **Shift** and an arrow key
  move it more. Options sets the distances.
- **Object Properties > Position and size** sets the place, width and
  height as numbers. **Keep proportions** keeps the shape.
- **H** and **V** mirror the selection; **Ctrl+R** turns it 90°. See
  [Arranging and the sew order](arranging) for all the ways to turn,
  align and copy.
- Right-click the selection for a menu of the edit and arrange commands.

## Hide and show

- **Alt+V** hides the selected objects on the screen, to see what is under
  them. They still sew. Alt+V with nothing selected shows all objects
  again.
- **View > Filter** shows only some objects: **Only These Colors**,
  **Hide Others** and **Show Colors** (a list with a box for each color).
  **Show All** shows all again.
- **View > Show** turns on and off the parts of the picture: the object
  outlines (**Artwork**), the **Stitches**, the **Thread Look**, the
  **Jumps**, the **Needle Points**, the **Trims and Colors** symbols, the
  **Appliqué Fabric**, the **Vectors** and the **Density Map**.

## Grid, rulers and guides

- **View > Grid** shows a grid. Options sets the spacing and a major line.
- **View > Rulers** shows rulers at the top and the left side. Drag from a
  ruler to make a guide. **Remove Guides** removes all guides.
- **Snap to Grid**, **Snap to Guides** and **Snap to Objects** make points
  and moves go to the grid, to the guides, or to the nodes of other
  objects.
- **View > Measure** measures a distance and an angle. Drag.

## Command windows

The windows of commands (for example Border trace, Offsets, Lettering,
Monogram and Fabric) dock at a corner of the canvas, as in CAD programs:

- A command window opens docked at the top left corner of the canvas.
- Drag it by its title, or by an empty place in it. Release it near a
  corner of the canvas: it docks at that corner. Release it at another
  place: it stays there.
- A docked window stays at its corner when the canvas changes size, for
  example when a panel opens.
- Two windows at one corner go one under the other.

## The workspace

- **View > Panels** opens and closes each panel: Object Properties,
  Styles, Design info, Clipart, Sequence, Threads, Stitch list, Exports
  and Library.
- Drag the tab of a panel to put it in another place, or next to another
  panel.
- **View > Dark Mode** changes between light and dark panels.
- **View > Options** has the other workspace settings. See
  [Options](options).
