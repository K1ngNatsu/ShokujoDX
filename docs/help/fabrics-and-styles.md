# Fabrics, styles and templates

## The fabric

**Design > Fabric** sets the fabric of the design. The fabric sets the
density, the pull compensation and the underlay of satin and fill
stitches, and the backing and topping on the worksheet.

The fabrics are: Woven cotton; Denim and canvas; T-shirt knit; Piqué knit
(polo shirts); Fleece and sweatshirt; Towel (terry); Felt; Leather and
vinyl; Silk and thin woven; Structured cap; Water-soluble stabilizer
(free-standing lace). **Not set** uses the base settings.

When you change the fabric:

- New objects always get the settings of the fabric.
- The window asks about the existing objects: each object keeps its own
  settings, changed by the difference between the two fabrics. Objects
  with no underlay or with a custom underlay keep their underlay.
- The **Backing** and the **Topping** go on the worksheet.

## Current properties

The **current properties** are the stitch settings that new objects get.
The property bar changes them while a drawing tool is active, and the
Styles panel shows them.

- **Make Current** (Design > Fabric and Styles): new objects get the
  settings of the selected objects.
- **Apply Current**: the selected objects get the current properties.

## Styles

A style is a set of stitch settings with a name. **Design > Styles**
shows the Styles panel:

- **New style** makes a style from the settings of the selected objects.
  Type a **Style name** first.
- **Apply** gives the selected objects the settings of the style. The
  **Style** list of the Design tab does the same.
- **Update** takes the settings of the selected objects into the style.
  Objects keep their settings until you apply it again.
- **Delete** removes the style.

The styles are part of the design.

## Favorite styles

Click the star of a style to make it a **favorite**. A favorite is for all
designs: **Design > Favorites** applies it to the selection in any design.

## Templates

A template gives a new design its machine, hoop, fabric, current
properties, styles, threads and notes.

- **New from Template** (Design tab, File menu or the start page) starts a
  new design from a template. **Normal** has the built-in settings. **Use
  for New** makes a template the one that **New** uses.
- **Lace ornament** and **Lace edge** start a free-standing lace design.
  See [Tutorial: a lace ornament](tutorial-lace).
- **Save as Template** saves the settings of this design as a template.
  **Keep the objects too** keeps the objects, for example a frame or a
  name badge that each new design starts with.

## My assets

**Design > My Assets** (or the File menu) shows your own templates,
embroidery fonts and alphabets, motifs, thread charts, clipart and hoops,
for all designs.

- **Import** and **Export** each kind, or **Open folder**.
- **Export all as a package** writes one zip file with all your assets,
  your favorite styles and your stones, for another computer or another
  user.
- **Import a package** puts the assets of a package into your folders.
