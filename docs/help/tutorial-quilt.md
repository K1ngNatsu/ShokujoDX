# Tutorial: a quilt block

In this tutorial you quilt round a heart with echo lines, fill the space
round it with a stipple background, and make a quilt block from a wave
line.

## Open the start design

Click [Open the start design](sample:quilt-heart). It has a red heart (a
fill) and a red wave line under it: the pattern of the quilt block.

## The fabric

1. Click **Design > Fabric and Styles > Fabric**.
2. In the **Fabric** list, select **Quilt (top, batting and backing)**,
   and click **Apply**. The layers of a quilt are thick and soft: the
   program uses long stitches for the quilting lines, and the 3D view
   shows the batting rise between them.

## Echo lines

Echo lines follow the outline of a shape, one outside the other, as one
line with no trims.

1. Click the heart to select it.
2. Click **Specialty > Quilting > Quilting**. The **Quilting** window
   opens on **Echo**.
3. Set **Distance** to 3 mm (from the heart to the first line), **Spacing**
   to 5 mm (between the lines) and **Lines** to 3.
4. Click **Add echo lines**.

![The Quilting window](quilt-window)

## A background

A stipple background fills the space round the shapes with a line that
wanders and does not cross itself. The shapes stand up from it.

1. Click the heart to select it again.
2. Click **Background** in the window. Keep the pattern **Stipple**.
3. Set **Gap** to 16 mm: the stipple starts outside the echo lines.
4. Click **Add background**.

## A quilt block

A quilt block repeats the selected lines over a rectangle. A block
larger than the hoop sews in hoopings, and the lines of two hoopings
meet.

1. Click the wave line to select it.
2. Click **Quilt block** in the window.
3. Set **Width** to 110 mm and **Height** to 50 mm.
4. Click **Make quilt block**. The wave repeats over the block, with a
   line round it.
5. Close the window. Click **View > Show > Thread Look**, and select
   **Thread Look** in its menu.

![The heart with echo lines, the stipple and the quilt block](quilt-stitches)

## If it does not look right

- **Add echo lines is gray.** Select the heart first.
- **The stipple covers the echo lines.** Make the **Gap** larger than the
  distance of the last echo line.
- **The block has gaps between the waves.** Set **Gap across** to 0, and
  draw a line whose ends are at the same height.
