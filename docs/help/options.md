# Options

**View > Options** (or **File > Options**) opens the Options window.
Changes apply at once and stay for the next start. **Reset to defaults**
sets all options back.

## Appearance and language

- **Appearance**: **Dark** or **Light** panels. **High contrast**: black
  and white panels with strong lines, for low vision.
- **Text size**: the size of all text and buttons. **Ctrl+Plus**,
  **Ctrl+Minus** and **Ctrl+0** change it too.
- **Language**: English, Deutsch, Español or 日本語. The help topics are
  in English.
- **Tools**: **All tools**, or **Simple Mode** (fewer tools, larger
  buttons).

## Units, grid and guides

- **Units**: **Millimeters** or **Inches**.
- **Grid**: **Show** and **Snap to Grid**; the **Grid spacing** and
  **Major line every**.
- **Rulers and guides**: **Show rulers** and **Snap to Guides**; the
  **Snap distance**.
- **Arrow key nudge** and **Shift + arrow nudge**: how far the arrow keys
  move the selection.

## Safety

- **Automatic backup**: designs with changes go to a backup folder every
  1 to 60 minutes (2 at the start), or **off**. After a crash, the program offers to recover them.
- **Hoop safety margin**: the distance inside the hoop field. Stitches
  outside it get a warning.

## Editing

- **Paste (Ctrl+V)**: where Paste puts the copies (the same place, moved,
  the hoop center or the stitch cursor).
- **Duplicate offset**: how far right and down a duplicate goes.
- **Reshape**: **Show the handles of all nodes** (else only the selected
  node shows its handles).
- **Zoom in and out by**: the zoom step of Zoom in and Zoom out.

## Display colors

The colors of the **Grid**, the **Guides**, the **Hoop** and the
**Selection** on the canvas. Set **Own color** to choose a color; else
the color of the theme shows.

## Density and thread

- **Density limit**: the number of layers of thread that the density map
  and Design info allow (5 at the start).
- **Top thread spool** and **Bobbin**: the length and the **price** of
  one, and the **Currency** (the text before the cost, for example $ or
  €). Design info and the worksheet then show the thread cost. A price of
  0 gives no cost.

## Screen calibration

Hold a ruler to the screen. Change the **Scale** until the line is
100 mm long, or type the length you measure in **Measured** and click
**Apply**. Then **Zoom 100 %** shows the real size. **Reset** sets the
value of the operating system again.

## Settings in other places

- The machine times and DST trims: **Design > Machine Settings**. See
  [Machines and hoops](machines-and-hoops).
- The hoop placement and the auto start and end: **Design > Hoop Options**.
- The shaping options: **Artwork > Shaping**.
- The files of Output design: the Output design window. See [Files and
  output](files-and-output).
