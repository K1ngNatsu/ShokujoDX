# Tutorial: rhinestones

In this tutorial you put rhinestones on the outline of a heart, add a
row of stones by hand, and make the marks that put the template in
place.

Rhinestones (hotfix stones) do not sew: you put them on the fabric with
a template and a heat press. The program places the stones, makes the
template for a cutter, and sews marks that show where the template goes.

## Open the start design

Click [Open the start design](sample:applique-heart). It has a red heart
(a fill).

## Stones on the outline

1. Click **Edit > Select > Select All**.
2. Click **Specialty > Rhinestones > Outline to Stones**. The heart is a
   row of stones on its outline now. The stones do not sew, so the status
   line shows no colors.
3. In Object Properties, at **Stone**, select **SS16 (3.9 mm)**: a larger
   stone. SS is the size of the stone; SS10 is 2.8 mm.
4. Set **Gap** to 1 mm: the space between two stones.

![The heart in rhinestones](rhinestones-heart)

Stones that overlap show as red rings. **Remove Overlaps** takes them
away.

## A row of stones by hand

1. Click **Specialty > Rhinestones > Stone Run**.
2. Click two points under the heart, and push **Enter**. A row of stones
   goes along the line. (A right click makes a curve point.)

**Stone** places one stone at each click, and **Stone Fill** fills an
area with rows or rings of stones.

## The template and its marks

1. Click **Specialty > Rhinestones > Template Marks**. The design sews
   four crosses round the stones, in the last color, "Template marks
   (pull out)". Sew them, put the template on the crosses, press the
   stones, and pull the crosses out.
2. Click **Specialty > Rhinestones > Template**, and save the template:
   SVG or DXF for a cutting machine, or PDF to print at full size.

![The stones and the template marks](rhinestones-marks)

## If it does not look right

- **Outline to Stones is gray.** Select a shape first.
- **Stones overlap at sharp corners.** Make the **Gap** larger, or keep
  **Remove Overlaps** on.
- **The template does not fit the stones.** Set **Template holes +** in
  the property bar of the stone tools: the holes of the template are
  that much larger than the stones.
