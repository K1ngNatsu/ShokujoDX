# Tutorial: cross stitch

In this tutorial you make machine cross stitch from a picture, on Aida
cloth, then add and erase crosses by hand, add a backstitch line, and
make the chart.

Cross stitch is a grid of cells. Each cell has a cross (or a part of a
cross) in one thread color. The machine sews the crosses; the chart is
for counted cross stitch by hand.

## Open the start design

Click [Open the start design](sample:patch-picture). It has a picture: a
navy circle with a yellow star.

## The fabric

1. Click **Design > Fabric and Styles > Fabric**.
2. In the **Fabric** list, select **Aida cloth (cross stitch)**, and click
   **Apply**. The 3D view shows the weave of Aida, with its holes.

## Picture to Cross Stitch

1. Click **Specialty > Cross Stitch > Picture to Cross Stitch**. The
   **Picture to Design** wizard opens, set to make cross stitch.
2. Click **Next ›**, then **Remove the edge color**: the white round the
   circle goes.
3. Click **Next ›**. In the **Cross Stitch** step, set **Cell size** to
   1.81 mm: the cells of Aida 14 (14 cells for each inch). The arrow next
   to **Cell size** has the usual sizes, for example **Aida 14 (1.81
   mm)**.
4. Set **Thread colors** to 2.

![The Cross Stitch step of the wizard](cross-stitch-wizard)

5. Click **Next ›**, then **Finish**. Each color is a cross-stitch object.

## Crosses by hand

The tools of **Specialty > Cross Stitch** work on the grid of the
selected cross-stitch object.

1. Click **Specialty > Cross Stitch > Cross Stitch**, and drag under the
   circle: each cell under the pointer gets a cross, in the current
   thread.
2. Click **Specialty > Cross Stitch > Erase Cells**, and drag over the
   first crosses of the row: they go. (Shift and a drag with the Cross
   Stitch tool does the same.)
3. Click **Specialty > Cross Stitch > Backstitch**, and click two corners
   of cells under the row: a backstitch line goes from corner to corner.
   Push **Esc** to end it.

The property bar of the Cross Stitch tool has the part of a cross: a full
cross, a half cross, a quarter or a three-quarter cross.

![The star in cross stitch, with the row and the backstitch line](cross-stitch-stitches)

## The chart

Click **Specialty > Cross Stitch > Chart**, and save the chart (a PDF).
Its first page has the size of the design on Aida 11, 14, 16 and 18 and
the key of the symbols; the other pages have the grid with a symbol in
each cell.

## If it does not look right

- **The crosses are too large.** The cell size of the grid is too large.
  Select a cross-stitch object, and set its **Cell size** in Object
  Properties: all objects on the grid change.
- **The tools start a new grid.** Select a cross-stitch object first, so
  the tools use its grid.
- **The star has a white edge.** Go back to the **Background** step, and
  remove the edge color.
