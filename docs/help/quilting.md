# Quilting

**Specialty > Quilting** opens the Quilting window. It has three parts:
**Echo**, **Background** and **Quilt block**. Quilting stitches are
lines, so they sew fast and keep the quilt soft.

## Echo

Lines round the selected shapes, at a distance, as one line:

- **Distance**: the distance of the first line from the shapes.
- **Spacing**: the distance between two lines.
- **Lines**: the number of lines.
- **Triple stitch**: each stitch three times, so the line shows more.

Select a border shape too (a fill or a vector that holds the others), and
the lines stop at its edge; the steps from one line to the next go along
the border. Click **Add echo lines**.

## Background

A stipple or meander fill of the area round the selected shapes and
appliqué, with a gap round them, so they stand up:

- **Pattern**: **Stipple**, or a meander of squares, triangles or
  hexagons.
- **Spacing**: the distance between the lines of the fill.
- **Gap**: the space between the shapes and the fill.
- **Margin**: with no border shape, how far the box goes past the shapes.

The area is the border shape, if you select one, or else a box round the
shapes. Click **Add background**.

## Quilt block

The selected lines repeated over a block, or a meander over it, cut at
the block edge:

- **Pattern**: **Selected lines**, or a meander.
- **Width** and **Height** of the block (300 × 300 mm at the start).
- **Gap across** and **Gap down**: the space between two copies side by
  side and between two rows. 0 across: the copies join.
- **Line round the block**.

Click **Make quilt block**.

Make a pattern whose end is level with its start, and set the gap across
to 0: the copies then join into long lines. A block larger than the hoop
sews in hoopings (see [Designs larger than the hoop](hooping)): the
lines are cut where two hoopings meet, so they join with no gap.

## Tips

- Use a quilting thread and a quilting needle, and a hoop with a strong
  grip, or a quilting frame.
- Hoop the quilt sandwich (top, batting and back) flat. Do not stretch
  it.
- The Meander fill type of Object Properties also fills one shape with a
  meander. See [Stitch types](stitch-types).
