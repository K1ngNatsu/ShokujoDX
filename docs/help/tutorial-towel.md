# Tutorial: a monogram on a towel

In this tutorial you put a monogram on a towel. The towel has a high pile,
so the design needs a laydown and the correct fabric settings.

You need a towel, a tear-away or cut-away backing, and a water-soluble
topping.

## Set the fabric

1. Click [Open the start design](sample:towel). It is an empty design.
2. Click **Design > Fabric and Styles > Fabric**.
3. In the **Fabric** list, select **Towel (terry)**, and click **Apply**.
   The program makes the underlay heavier and the stitches denser, so
   they stay on top of the pile. The worksheet shows the backing and the
   topping.

## Make the monogram

1. Click **Lettering > Personalize > Monogram**.
2. Type the **Initials**, for example `JMK`.
3. Click the template **Stacked in an oval**. It sets the style, the
   size, the border and the ornaments. You can change each of them
   after.
4. Click **Add to the design**. The monogram is on the canvas, and the
   window changes the selected monogram now.
5. Close the **Monogram** window.

![The Monogram window](towel-monogram)

## Add the laydown

1. Click **Edit > Select > Select All**.
2. Click **Specialty > Puff and Laydown > Laydown**. A light fill goes
   under the monogram. It sews first, in a thread of the fabric color, so
   it does not show. It presses the pile down.
3. Push **Esc**.

## Check it

1. Click **View > Show > Thread Look**, and select **Thread Look** in its
   menu. Under the fabric color (**Color > Fabric Color**), the fabric
   texture of the towel shows.
2. The status line shows **Colors: 3** (the laydown, the letters and the
   border) and no warning.
3. **Design > Product > Product** shows the design on a towel at real
   size.

![The monogram on the towel fabric](towel-stitches)

## Sew it

1. Hoop the towel with the backing. Do not stretch the towel.
2. Put the water-soluble topping on top of the towel, in the hoop.
3. Sew the design. Tear away the backing, and wash or tear away the
   topping.

## If it does not look right

- **The pile comes up through the letters.** Use the topping, and keep
  the laydown. The fabric **Towel (terry)** must be set.
- **The status line shows "Thread builds up".** Letters or a border lie
  on top of each other. Make the monogram larger, or select another
  template.
- **The laydown shows.** Give the laydown thread the color of the towel.
