# Tutorial: a monogram on a towel

In this tutorial you put a monogram on a towel. The towel has a high pile,
so the design needs a laydown and the correct fabric settings. It takes
about 10 minutes.

You need a towel, a tear-away or cut-away backing, and a water-soluble
topping.

## Set the fabric

1. Start a new design.
2. On the **Design** tab, select your machine and a hoop.
3. Click **Design > Fabric** and select **Towel (terry)**. The program
   makes the underlay heavier and the stitches denser, so they stay on
   top of the pile. The worksheet shows the backing and the topping.

## Make the monogram

1. Click **Lettering > Monogram**.
2. Type the **Initials**, for example `JMK`.
3. Select a **Style**, for example **Classic (large middle)**, and set
   the **Height** to 60 mm.
4. Under **Border**, select a **Shape** (for example **Circle**) and the
   **Stitch** **Satin**.
5. Add **Ornaments** if you want, for example a **Leaf** at the left and
   right.
6. Click **Add as new**. The monogram is on the canvas.

## Add the laydown

1. Select all objects (Ctrl+A).
2. Click **Specialty > Laydown**. A light fill goes under the monogram. It
   sews first, in a thread of its own.
3. Give the laydown a thread of the towel color, so it does not show.

## Check it

1. Click **View > Thread Look** to see the threads on the fabric. The
   fabric texture of the towel shows under the fabric color (**Color > Fabric Color**).
2. Look at **Design Info**: it has the size, the sew time and the
   warnings.
3. **Design > Product** shows the design on a towel at real size.

## Sew it

1. Hoop the towel with the backing. Do not stretch the towel.
2. Put the water-soluble topping on top of the towel, in the hoop.
3. Sew the design. Tear away the backing, and wash or tear away the
   topping.
