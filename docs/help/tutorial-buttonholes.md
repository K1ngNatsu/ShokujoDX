# Tutorial: buttonholes

In this tutorial you place a string of four buttonholes on the placket
of a shirt, for buttons of a standard size, and then change them.

## Open the start design

Click [Open the start design](sample:placket). It has a navy line of 80
mm: the middle of the placket, where the buttonholes go.

## Place the buttonholes

1. Click the line to select it.
2. Click **Digitize > Decorate > Buttonholes**. The **Buttonholes**
   window opens.
3. At **Shape**, keep **Square ends**. **Keyhole** is for jackets and
   coats, **Stretch (for knits)** for knit fabric, and **Eyelet** makes a
   round hole for a cord.
4. In the **Button** list (**Standard sizes**), select **18 mm (28L)**. A
   button size is the diameter of the button; the opening is the
   diameter and the thickness of the button.
5. Set **Button thickness** to 3 mm. The **Opening** is now 21 mm.
6. The line is selected, so the window can place a string of
   buttonholes along it. Set **Number** to 4.
7. Click **Place buttonholes**. Four buttonholes go across the line, at
   the same distance from each other. The line is used up.

![The Buttonholes window](buttonholes-window)

Each buttonhole is a running box and satin columns: two legs and two bar
tacks. The four buttonholes are one group, and they are selected.

## Change the buttonholes

1. With the buttonholes selected, click **Mitre corners**: the legs and
   the bar tacks become one column that turns at the corners.
2. Click **Change the selected buttonholes**.
3. Close the window. Click **View > Show > Thread Look**, and select
   **Thread Look** in its menu.

![Four buttonholes on the placket](buttonholes-stitches)

## Sew and cut

Sew the buttonholes on the fabric of the garment with a firm
stabilizer. Then cut each opening with a buttonhole cutter or a seam
ripper, from the ends to the middle, and put a pin at each bar tack so
that the cut does not go through it.

## If it does not look right

- **The window places one buttonhole only.** No line is selected. Select
  the line first, then open the window.
- **The opening is too small for the button.** Set the **Button
  thickness** of a thick button, or set the **Opening** by hand.
- **The legs are too dense on a light fabric.** Open **Stitches**, and
  lower the **Density**.
