# Tutorial: a photo in stitches

In this tutorial you make a photo stitch from a photo with **Picture to
Design**. A photo stitch is rows of stitches: the rows are dense where
the photo is dark and open where it is light. It is not made of flat
areas of color, as a logo is.

You need no file: the start design has a photo of an apple. With your
own photo, use a photo with a clear subject, good light and a plain
background. Crop it to the subject first (**Artwork > Picture**).

## Open the start design

Click [Open the start design](sample:photo-apple). The photo shows as a
backdrop picture, dimmed under the design.

![The photo of an apple](photo-start)

## Picture to Design

1. Click **Artwork > Import > Picture to Design**.
2. At **Make**, select **Photo stitch (a portrait, a photo)**.
3. Click **Next ›**. In the **Background** step, click **Remove the edge
   color**: the light background goes. For a photo with a busy
   background, use **Cut out subject**: drag a box round the subject.
4. Click **Next ›**. The **Photo stitch** step shows the rows on the
   right.
5. At **Style**, select **Color rows**, and set **Thread colors** to 3.
   - **Rows (one thread)**: one dark thread on a light fabric.
   - **Color rows**: rows in some thread colors of the photo.
   - **Scribble (one thread)**: a line that goes to and fro, dense where
     the photo is dark.

![The Photo stitch step](photo-step)

6. **Row spacing** sets the detail: smaller gives more detail and more
   stitches. **Contrast** and **Brightness** change the light and dark
   before the rows are made.
7. Click **Next ›**, then **Finish**. The photo stitch is on the canvas.
   Push **Esc**.

## Look at the stitches

Click **View > Show > Thread Look**, and select **Thread Look** in its
menu.

![The apple in color rows](photo-stitches)

A photo stitch has many stitches. Sew it on a stable fabric with a firm
cut-away stabilizer, and sew a test first.

## If it does not look right

- **It is too dark, or the fabric puckers.** Make **Row spacing** larger,
  or lower the **Contrast**.
- **The background sews.** Go back to the **Background** step (click
  **‹ Back**) and remove more of it.
- **The details are lost.** Make **Row spacing** smaller, or make the
  design larger (**Design width** in the **Photo stitch** step).
